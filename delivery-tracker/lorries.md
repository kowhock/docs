# Lorries

Which driver is on which lorry, what each lorry is doing today, and when it's in for maintenance. The lorry number then shows next to the driver's name everywhere in the app and on the trip form.

## The page at a glance

![Lorries page with lorry cards](./img/lorries.webp)

<div class="callouts">

- <span class="n">1</span> **+ Add lorry.** Add a lorry to your branch.
- <span class="n">2</span> **What it's doing today:** On the road (with the DO), jobs left today, done today, available, no driver, or under maintenance.
- <span class="n">3</span> **Driver.** Pick who drives this lorry. Each lorry has one driver, and each driver one lorry.
- <span class="n">4</span> **🔧 Maintenance.** Book the lorry in for maintenance.
- <span class="n">5</span> **History.** Driver changes and maintenance, with who did it and when.
- <span class="n">6</span> A lorry **under maintenance** has a yellow border and shows until when.
- <span class="n">7</span> **✅ Back in service** when it's ready again.

</div>

## Add a lorry

![Add lorry form](./img/lorry-add.webp)

<div class="callouts">

- <span class="n">1</span> **Lorry number**, the number painted on the lorry (for example 46).
- <span class="n">2</span> **Plate number**, for example JQN 9833.
- <span class="n">3</span> **Branch** it belongs to.
- <span class="n">4</span> **Save.** Then pick its driver on the card.

</div>

Use **Edit** on a card to change the number, plate or branch.

## Change a lorry's driver

Pick a different name in the card's **Driver** box. If that changes something else, the app asks first.

![Change driver confirmation](./img/lorry-change-driver.webp)

<div class="callouts">

- <span class="n">1</span> What will happen, for example the lorry's current driver comes off it, or the new driver leaves their old lorry.
- <span class="n">2</span> **Yes, change** to go ahead, or **Cancel** to keep things as they are.

</div>

## Maintenance

Tap **🔧 Maintenance** on the lorry's card.

![Maintenance form](./img/lorry-maintenance.webp)

<div class="callouts">

- <span class="n">1</span> **From.** The day it goes in (can be a future date to book ahead).
- <span class="n">2</span> **Back in service**, if you know it. Leave empty if not.
- <span class="n">3</span> **What's being done**, for example "Service + tyres". This shows on the card.
- <span class="n">4</span> If a driver is still on this lorry, the form reminds you. To keep them delivering, put them on another lorry from that lorry's card.
- <span class="n">5</span> **Save.**

</div>

When it's fixed, tap **✅ Back in service** on the card. Use **Change dates** if the repair takes longer.
