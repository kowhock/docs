# Common problems

### A DO from AutoCount isn't showing
DOs come in from AutoCount every 5 minutes. Check **AutoCount synced … ago** under the Deliveries title. If it says more than about 15 minutes, the office PC that runs the sync may be off. Tell the admin.

Also check the filters: the DO may be on another date (try **All dates** or search the DO number), another branch, or hidden because it's **O/C** (tap **Show O/C**).

### I can't assign a driver to a DO
- The DO is delivered by another branch. Only that branch can plan it. You'll see "… is delivering this DO" on the card.
- The driver has already started or delivered it.

### I can't drag a DO on Driver status
DOs that are **Out for delivery** or **Delivered** stay where they are, so the driver's order doesn't change under them. DOs another branch delivers can't be moved either.

### A DO has a red "Set points" chip
The map couldn't find its address. Click the chip and type an address with the taman or town, or key in the points. See [Fix an address or points](./driver-status#fix-an-address-or-points).

### The Outstanding tab is red
Some DOs still need fixing in AutoCount. Open [Outstanding](./outstanding), fix each one in AutoCount, then tap **Clear**.

### The monthly trip form lost my remarks
Remarks on the monthly form aren't saved. Type them and export the PDF or Excel straight away. See [Monthly trip form](./trip-form#monthly-trip-form).

### A driver forgot their PIN
The admin resets it. Tell the driver the new 6-digit PIN.

### A driver doesn't see a DO on their phone
- Check the DO is **assigned to them** and dated **today** on Deliveries.
- The phone checks for changes every minute. To check now, close and reopen the app.
- Check they're signed in as themselves (their name is at the top: "Hi, …").

### The WhatsApp button doesn't show
It only shows when the DO has a Malaysian **mobile** number. Office numbers and missing numbers have no WhatsApp button, but **Call** still works if there's a number.
