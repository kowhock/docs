# Outstanding

Every DO that still needs fixing in AutoCount, in one list. Remarks are added from the DO number on [Deliveries](./deliveries#add-a-remark-outstanding).

## The list

![Outstanding page with open remarks](./img/outstanding.webp)

<div class="callouts">

- <span class="n">1</span> The **Outstanding** tab is **red** with a count while anything isn't cleared. It turns **green** when everything is done.
- <span class="n">2</span> **Not cleared / Cleared / All.** It opens on what still needs doing.
- <span class="n">3</span> **Branch and dates.** The dates are the day the remark was added. Tap **Go** to filter.
- <span class="n">4</span> **What to do** in AutoCount: Add Credit Note (CN), Edit DO, Add Delivery Return (DR), or your own text.
- <span class="n">5</span> **Clear.** Tap after you've fixed it in AutoCount.

</div>

Each row shows the DO number, the reason, the description, the customer, the DO date, and who added it and when.

## After clearing

![Cleared remarks with a green Outstanding tab](./img/outstanding-cleared.webp)

<div class="callouts">

- <span class="n">1</span> With nothing left, the tab is green.
- <span class="n">2</span> The **Cleared** tab shows when each one was cleared and by whom.

</div>

::: tip Daily habit
Check the Outstanding tab before going home. If it's red, something still needs fixing in AutoCount.
:::
