# Driver phone view

What drivers see on their phone: today's DOs, trip by trip, in the order the office planned. See [Signing in](./sign-in) for the PIN keypad and adding the app to the home screen.

## Today's deliveries

<img src="./img/driver-home.webp" alt="Phone: driver home screen" width="360">

<div class="callouts">

- <span class="n">1</span> **EN / BM.** Switch between English and Bahasa Melayu. The phone remembers it.
- <span class="n">2</span> **Day.** It always opens on today. **‹ ›** go to the day before or after, or pick a date and tap **Go**.
- <span class="n">3</span> **Progress** for the day: how many DOs are delivered.
- <span class="n">4</span> **Today's commission.** Points earned so far and how many more to the next ringgit. Tap **How it works** for the rules.
- <span class="n">5</span> **Trip 1.** DOs are grouped by trip, first trip first.
- <span class="n">6</span> **A DO card**: DO number and points, customer, address, estimated time and the office's notes.

</div>

<img src="./img/driver-trip-later.webp" alt="Phone: a later trip, folded" width="360">

<div class="callouts">

- <span class="n">1</span> A later trip stays **folded** until the trip before it is finished. Tap it to look inside anyway.

</div>

## Start a delivery

<img src="./img/driver-job-start.webp" alt="Phone: DO card ready to start" width="360">

<div class="callouts">

- <span class="n">1</span> **Estimated time** the office gave the customer.
- <span class="n">2</span> **Notes** from the sales order, for example where to unload.
- <span class="n">3</span> **Start delivery** when you leave for this customer.

</div>

## On the way

<img src="./img/driver-job-on-the-way.webp" alt="Phone: DO out for delivery" width="360">

<div class="callouts">

- <span class="n">1</span> **Waze / Maps** open directions to the address. **Call** phones the customer.
- <span class="n">2</span> **Items.** Tap to see what's on the DO.
- <span class="n">3</span> **Tell customer I'm on the way** opens WhatsApp with a message already written. Just tap send.
- <span class="n">4</span> **Can't deliver** if the delivery can't happen. See [below](#can-t-deliver).
- <span class="n">5</span> **Delivered** when the goods are handed over.

</div>

## Delivered: take the DO photo

After tapping **Delivered**:

<img src="./img/driver-deliver-photo.webp" alt="Phone: photo and note before confirming" width="360">

<div class="callouts">

- <span class="n">1</span> **Take photo of signed DO / goods.** Opens the camera. The photo is made smaller first to save mobile data.
- <span class="n">2</span> **Received by / note** (optional), for example who signed.
- <span class="n">3</span> **Confirm.**

</div>

<img src="./img/driver-delivered.webp" alt="Phone: delivered DO with WhatsApp button" width="360">

<div class="callouts">

- <span class="n">1</span> The DO is now **Delivered**.
- <span class="n">2</span> **Send "delivered" WhatsApp** opens WhatsApp with a thank-you message to the customer.

</div>

The office sees it at the same moment. The card moves to **Delivered** on their Deliveries page without refreshing:

<img src="./img/office-sees-delivered.webp" alt="Office Deliveries page showing the DO as delivered" width="346">

<div class="callouts">

- <span class="n">1</span> The DO the driver just delivered, now in the **Delivered** column.

</div>

The DO photo is saved with the DO. Admins can see it on the sales order's page.

## Can't deliver

<img src="./img/driver-cant-deliver.webp" alt="Phone: choose why it couldn't be delivered" width="360">

<div class="callouts">

- <span class="n">1</span> **Why**: customer not available, shop closed, wrong address, customer rejected goods, vehicle problem, or **Other** (then type what happened).
- <span class="n">2</span> **Mark failed.** The office sees the reason and will reschedule it.

</div>

## Bahasa Melayu

<img src="./img/driver-bm.webp" alt="Phone: driver view in Bahasa Melayu" width="360">

<div class="callouts">

- <span class="n">1</span> Tap **BM** for Bahasa Melayu or **EN** for English. Failure reasons are still saved in English for the office.

</div>
