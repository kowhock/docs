# Points & commission

Drivers earn points for each DO they deliver. Points turn into commission once they pass the daily target.

## Points per DO

| Delivery | Points |
|---|---|
| Up to 20 km from the store | **2** |
| Over 20 km from the store | **3** |
| Extra stops in the same trip | **1** each |

In each trip, **one DO gets the full points** (the one worth the most). Every other DO in that trip counts as an extra stop: 1 point. The DO card shows this as **1 pt · extra stop (2 alone)**.

Distance is the driving distance from the delivering branch's store, worked out automatically from the address. If the map can't find the address, the office [types an address or the points](./driver-status#fix-an-address-or-points).

## Commission per day

- The target is **10 points a day**.
- From the 10th point, every point is **RM 5**.

| Points that day | Commission |
|---|---|
| 9 or less | RM 0 |
| 10 | RM 5 |
| 12 | RM 15 |
| 14 | RM 25 |

Points count on the day the DO was **delivered**, not its planned date.
